Daria Orlova

Transdisciplinary artist, performer, and curator. Works with sound and practices of attentive listening

Her artistic, research, and pedagogical work is aimed at reassembling and co-tuning cultural communities and micro-groups in Saint Petersburg through the organisation of sound events — concerts, performances, workshops, lectures, and walks. These practices help create a more inclusive auditory space, strengthening trust and developing a sense of belonging.
