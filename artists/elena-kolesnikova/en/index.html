Elena Kolesnikova

Artist, architect, curator of a home residency

She researches residential spaces and architecture as socially embedded environments. As a curator, she works with the local artistic community, collectively creating horizontal connections and engaging with contexts that are meaningful to them. As an artist, she creates social sculptures, installations, and graphic works.
