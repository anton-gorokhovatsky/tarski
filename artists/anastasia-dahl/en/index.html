Anastasia Dahl

Dance and performance artist

She works with dance performance and, beyond this medium, is interested in how the particular subtlety of attention trained through dance improvisation can manifest itself in other contexts. She is close to sociology and cultural anthropology. She also leads art mediations in art galleries and curates a community of interdisciplinary artists in Bristol.
